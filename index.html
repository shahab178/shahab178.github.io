About

I am a virologist and molecular biologist with over 10 years of experience spanning RNA virus biology, next-generation sequencing, and bioinformatics pipeline development. My work sits at the intersection of wet-lab virology and large-scale computational analysis, with a track record of 16 peer-reviewed publications across high-impact journals.

My doctoral research at the University of Sao Paulo dissected host immune responses during fatal Yellow Fever virus infection, using integrative RNA-seq to identify dysregulated innate immune and metabolic pathways in post-mortem clinical tissue. During my postdoctoral fellowship at Emory University, I designed in vivo CD8+ T-cell depletion experiments in ART-suppressed infant rhesus macaques to investigate pediatric HIV reservoir dynamics, and developed a CLSI-validated multiplex digital PCR assay (IPDA) for quantifying intact proviral DNA with Probit LOD95 documentation.

Beyond the bench, I care deeply about science education. I have taught undergraduate Virology laboratories, mentored a master's student from RNA extraction through phylogenetic analysis to independent publication, and guided a sabbatical postdoctoral researcher into viral metagenomics. I know firsthand how transformative the right mentorship can be at critical stages of a scientific career.

Research

🦠

Viral Genomics & Molecular Epidemiology

Genomic surveillance of RNA viruses including Zika, Dengue, Chikungunya, and Yellow Fever. Phylogeographic analysis of outbreak origins and transmission dynamics using Bayesian (BEAST2) and maximum-likelihood (IQ-TREE) methods.

🧬

Host-Pathogen Interactions & Transcriptomics

Integrative bulk RNA-seq to characterize host immune responses during fatal viral infections. Built a STAR/DESeq2 pipeline on post-mortem YFV liver tissue that identified 4,101 DEGs, revealing dysregulated type III interferon, inflammatory, and metabolic programs.

🔬

Pediatric HIV Reservoir Biology

In vivo CD8+ T-cell depletion in ART-suppressed infant rhesus macaques (barcoded SIVmac239M model) to define immune correlates of viral reservoir maintenance. CLSI-validated multiplex IPDA on the QIAcuity dPCR platform (Probit LOD95 ~6 c/rxn).

💻

Bioinformatics Pipeline Development

Reproducible pipelines for RNA-seq, viral metagenomics, and phylogenetics deployed on HPC environments. Tools: STAR, DESeq2, Kraken2, MetaSPAdes, BEAST2, IQ-TREE, iVar. Programming in R (advanced), Bash (advanced), Python, Nextflow.

🌍

Environmental & Viral Metagenomics

Comparative metagenomic characterization of DNA virus communities in freshwater and wastewater systems across anthropogenic gradients. Public-health oriented virome ecology using Kraken2, MetaSPAdes, PROKKA, and diversity metrics.

💉

Systems Vaccinology

Multi-omics integrative analysis of COVID-19 and Dengue vaccine responses. Co-contributor to published systems vaccinology atlases in Science Translational Medicine (2025) and Frontiers in Immunology (2024).
